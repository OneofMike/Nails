BEFORE YOU BOOK

Studio policies

Tap a topic to see the details and know what to expect before your appointment.

9 quick details ✦
💳 Deposits
A nonrefundable deposit is required to secure every appointment and is applied to your balance.
📅 Rescheduling
Please make changes at least 24 hours in advance. A new deposit may be required after that window.
⏰ Late arrivals
A grace period of 10 minutes is allowed. Later arrivals may require a simplified design or cancellation.
🚫 No-shows
No-show appointments forfeit the deposit and require full payment before booking again.
👯‍♀️ Guests
Please arrive alone unless a guest has been approved in advance. Space in the suite is limited.
🫧 Soak-offs
Book a soak-off if you arrive with existing product. Foreign work must be removed before a new set.
💵 Payments
Card, Apple Pay, and cash are accepted. Remaining balances are due at the end of the appointment.
🩹 Repairs
Contact the studio within 72 hours about lifting or breakage for a complimentary assessment.
🎀 Age requirement
Clients under 16 must have a parent or guardian approve the service before the appointment.
